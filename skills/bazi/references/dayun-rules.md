# Luck pillars (大运) and annual pillars (流年)

## Direction
Year-stem polarity: yang stems 甲丙戊庚壬, yin stems 乙丁己辛癸.

| Year stem | Sex | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| yang | male | forward |
| yang | female | backward |
| yin | male | backward |
| yin | female | forward |

Mnemonic: yang-male and yin-female go forward.

## Building the pillars
Start from the **month pillar**. Forward: move both stem and branch one step later in their cycles each time. Backward: one step earlier.
- Stems: 甲乙丙丁戊己庚辛壬癸 (cycles).
- Branches: 子丑寅卯辰巳午未申酉戌亥 (cycles).
- Example, month pillar 丙寅: forward 丁卯 → 戊辰 → 己巳 → 庚午 → ...; backward 乙丑 → 甲子 → 癸亥 → 壬戌 → ...

## Start age
Count days from birth to the **next 节** (forward) or **previous 节** (backward). Only the twelve 节 count (立春 惊蛰 清明 立夏 芒种 小暑 立秋 白露 寒露 立冬 大雪 小寒), not the 气 between them.
- `start age = days ÷ 3` (3 days = 1 year).
- Remainder: 1 day ≈ 4 months, 2 days ≈ 8 months, 0 = whole years. Round to the nearest year when stating the age.
- State the day count so the user can check it.

Each pillar governs ten years. Example (starts at 5, month pillar 丙寅, forward): ages 5-14 丁卯, 15-24 戊辰, 25-34 己巳, 35-44 庚午, and so on. Before the start age, use the month pillar itself.

## Annual pillars
The stem-branch of each year (year changes at 立春). Read them against the chart and the current luck pillar: luck pillar is the climate, the annual pillar is the trigger.
- Good luck pillar, poor year: steady overall, a bumpy year.
- Poor luck pillar, good year: hard period with an opening.
- Look for clashes, combinations, punishments and harms between the year and the chart.

## Notes
1. Years when one luck pillar hands over to the next tend to bring change.
2. One school says the stem governs the first five years and the branch the last five; another says both govern all ten. Name the school if it matters.
3. Judge each pillar against the chart's favourable and unfavourable elements: favourable arrives well, unfavourable arrives badly.
