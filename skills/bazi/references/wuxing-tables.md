## Element cycles
- Generates: wood → fire → earth → metal → water → wood.
- Controls: wood → earth → water → fire → metal → wood.

## Ten Gods (relative to the day stem)
| Relation to day master | Same polarity | Different polarity |
|---|---|---|
| Same element | 比肩 Friend | 劫财 Rob Wealth |
| I generate | 食神 Eating God | 伤官 Hurting Officer |
| I control | 偏财 Indirect Wealth | 正财 Direct Wealth |
| Controls me | 七杀 Seven Killings | 正官 Direct Officer |
| Generates me | 偏印 Indirect Resource | 正印 Direct Resource |

Typical meanings: 比肩 peers, rivals; 劫财 competitors, loss of money; 食神 talent, comfort, children (female chart); 伤官 skill, rebellion; 偏财 windfalls, father (male chart); 正财 salary, wife (male chart); 七杀 pressure, authority; 正官 status, rules, husband (female chart); 偏印 unusual learning, solitude; 正印 mother, study, helpers.

## Five-stem combinations (天干五合)
甲己 → earth; 乙庚 → metal; 丙辛 → water; 丁壬 → wood; 戊癸 → fire. A combination transforms only with the right season and branch roots; otherwise it is only "bound" (合绊).

## Branch relations
- **Clashes 六冲:** 子午, 丑未, 寅申, 卯酉, 辰戌, 巳亥.
- **Three-harmony 三合 (needs all three; two is a half-harmony):** 申子辰 water, 亥卯未 wood, 寅午戌 fire, 巳酉丑 metal.
- **Directional 三会 (stronger than 三合):** 寅卯辰 wood, 巳午未 fire, 申酉戌 metal, 亥子丑 water.
- **Six harmonies 六合:** 子丑 earth, 寅亥 wood, 卯戌 fire, 辰酉 metal, 巳申 water, 午未 fire (some say earth).
- **Punishments 刑:** 寅巳申, 丑戌未, 子卯; self-punishment 辰辰, 午午, 酉酉, 亥亥.
- **Harms 害:** 子未, 丑午, 寅巳, 卯辰, 申亥, 酉戌.
