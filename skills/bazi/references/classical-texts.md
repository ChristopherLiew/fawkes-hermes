# Classical texts: rule summaries

Paraphrased summaries for citing by book name. Cite only what appears here, and do not present a summary as a verbatim quote.

## 穷通宝典 Qiongtong Baojian (seasonal adjustment 调候)
- Read the season first: the chart needs warmth in winter and cooling in summer, then structure and strength.
- Summer fire is strong and wants water; winter water is strong and wants fire.
- Examples: 甲 born in the 寅 month wants 丙 for warmth, then 癸 for moisture. 甲 in the 酉 month (metal in command) wants 丁 to control metal, then 丙. 庚 in the 子 month wants 丙 to thaw it, and 甲 to feed 丁.
- If the seasonal-adjustment element is missing or damaged, even a good structure struggles. Check it first.

## 三命通会 Sanming Tonghui (structures and symbolic stars)
- Structures: 正官 upright, needs protection from 伤官; 七杀 forceful, wants 食神 to restrain it or 印 to transform it; 财 (wealth) fears rivals; 印 (resource) is learning, and 偏印 blocks 食神; 食神 is comfortable income; 伤官 is talent, and clashing with the Officer is a classic warning (with exceptions).
- Stars (神煞) are secondary aids, never the basis of a reading.
  - 天乙贵人 (noble helper), by day or year stem → branches: 甲戊庚 → 丑未; 乙己 → 子申; 丙丁 → 亥酉; 壬癸 → 卯巳; 辛 → 午寅.
  - 驿马 (travel horse), by year or day branch: 申子辰 → 寅; 寅午戌 → 申; 巳酉丑 → 亥; 亥卯未 → 巳.
  - 桃花 (peach blossom, charm): 申子辰 → 酉; 寅午戌 → 卯; 巳酉丑 → 午; 亥卯未 → 子.
  - 华盖 (canopy, solitude, study): 申子辰 → 辰; 寅午戌 → 戌; 巳酉丑 → 丑; 亥卯未 → 未.

## 滴天髓 Ditian Sui (strength and flow)
- Heaven (stems) shows outward expression, Earth (branches) shows foundation, Human is how they combine.
- Day-master strength: in command (month branch supports it, most important), rooted (supported by other branches), and with the tide (peers or resource stems help).
- Prefer flow between elements (metal → water → wood → fire). Blockages call for a mediating element.
- Very strong → drain, don't attack; very weak → don't prop up, follow the stronger force (follow structure).

## 渊海子平 Yuanhai Ziping (foundations)
- Ten Gods and relatives: Resource ↔ mother; Indirect Wealth ↔ father; Direct Wealth ↔ wife (male chart); Direct Officer ↔ husband (female chart); Eating God / Hurting Officer ↔ children (female chart); Friend / Rob Wealth ↔ siblings.
- Regular structures: the eight from the Ten Gods. Special: follow structures, transformation, 建禄, 羊刃.
- Choosing the useful element: support or restrain (strength), season adjustment, mediation, or follow the flow.

## 千里命稿 Qianli Minggao (practice)
- Judge structure level first, then whether the useful element is strong, then whether luck and years support it.
- Palaces: year = ancestors and early life (about 1-16); month = parents, siblings, work (17-32); day = self and spouse (33-48); hour = children, late life, results (49+). Age bands are one school's view.

## 协纪辨方书 Xieji Bianfang Shu (stars, date selection)
- Stars are looked up by year or day stem/branch. Lucky: 天乙, 天德, 月德, 文昌, 学堂. Unlucky: 羊刃, 亡神, 劫煞, 灾煞, 六厄, 孤辰, 寡宿.
- Never read stars alone. A lucky star that is attacked weakens; an unlucky one that is restrained does less harm.

## 子平真诠 Ziping Zhenquan (structure)
- A clean structure is better than a mixed one: one useful element is clear; several, or a clashed one, muddy it.
- Success: useful element strong, supported by an assisting element, harmful element restrained. Failure: useful element attacked, no support, harmful element in command. Mixed results need weighing.
- Useful (用神) maintains the structure; assisting (相神) supports it (for example Resource for a Direct Officer structure).

## 神峰通考 Shenfeng Tongkao (critique)
- "Illness and medicine": a chart's flaw is the illness, and a luck pillar or year that brings the remedy is where achievement comes.
- Skeptical of mechanically applying stars; the base is element interaction. Rules bend to the case.

## 果老星宗 Guolao Xingzong (star astrology)
- A different system (Life Palace and seven governors plus four remainders). Optional here, supporting only.
- Life Palace rule of thumb: from 寅 count the month forward to the birth double-hour; the palace it lands on is the Life Palace. Treat as a side note, not part of the main reading.
