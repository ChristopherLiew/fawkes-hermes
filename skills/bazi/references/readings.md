# Reading modes and best practice

## Which mode?
| User gives | Mode |
|---|---|
| Birth year only, or "my zodiac" | **Zodiac mode** (below). Offer a full chart if they share date and place. |
| Date, no time | **Three-pillar mode**: year, month, day. Reliable for temperament, strength, structure, luck pillars, years. Skip the hour pillar, children, late-life claims. |
| Date and exact time | **Full chart**. |
| Date and a rough time | Full chart for each plausible double-hour (usually 2-3), then say which conclusions are the same in all of them. |

## Birth time sensitivity
The hour pillar changes every two hours, so a wrong time changes one of four pillars and can change strength, structure and the favourable elements.
1. Ask for the time on the birth certificate or hospital record first; family memory comes second.
2. Check clock history before converting. Singapore and Malaysia clocks ran UTC+7:30 until 1982-01-01 (UTC+8 after). China used summer time in 1986-1991. Hong Kong used summer time until 1979. Convert records to standard time, and say you did.
3. True solar time (longitude plus the equation of time) can move a birth across a double-hour boundary. Within 20 minutes of a boundary, treat both hours as candidates.
4. **Rectification** when the time is uncertain: for each candidate hour, list 3-5 checkable past events (a move, a job change, a marriage, a loss), and let the user say which pillar set fits. A fit counts only when most events agree, not one. Present the result as "best fit", not certain.

## Zodiac mode (生肖)
- Animal comes from the year branch: 子 rat, 丑 ox, 寅 tiger, 卯 rabbit, 辰 dragon, 巳 snake, 午 horse, 未 goat, 申 monkey, 酉 rooster, 戌 dog, 亥 pig.
- Popular zodiac changes at Lunar New Year; BaZi changes at 立春 (Start of Spring). For births from about January to early February, show both and say which one this skill uses (立春).
- Add the stem element for the full sign (for example 甲辰 wood dragon, 庚午 metal horse).
- Give only broad temperament and the year outlook (below). Do not claim a precise reading from the animal alone; say a full chart needs date and ideally time.

## Annual outlook (流年) and Tai Sui (太岁)
For the year being asked about, take its branch (the "Tai Sui") and compare with the person's **year branch** (zodiac) and, in a full chart, the day branch too:
- **值太岁 (same):** the person's own year; often change and pressure.
- **冲 (clash):** opposite branch; movement, disruption.
- **刑 (punish), 害 (harm), 破 (break):** friction in work or relationships.
- **合 (combine):** support, but it can bind rather than help.
These are weaker than a full chart: a zodiac clash alone is not a bad year. In a full chart, weigh the annual pillar against the favourable elements and luck pillar instead (see `dayun-rules.md`).
Offer "remedies" only as practical habits (planning, rest, care in travel and money). No purchases, rituals or fees.

## 纳音 (sound element)
Optional flavour: each of the 60 pillars has a "sound element" (for example 甲子 gold-in-the-sea). It is a lighter, popular layer; label it so and do not let it override the chart.

## Output template
1. **Chart:** four pillars table (or three), with Ten Gods and hidden stems.
2. **At a glance:** day master, strength, favourable and unfavourable elements, structure, in 3-5 lines.
3. **Personality** (short), **career and money**, **relationships**, **health habits** (never diagnosis).
4. **Luck pillars:** a one-line summary per decade, current one expanded.
5. **This year and next:** annual pillar, Tai Sui relation, practical advice.
6. **Confidence:** which parts depend on the birth time or school choice.
7. The closing disclaimer line.

## Quality habits
- Say "tends to", "often", never "will". Offer more than one reading when the chart is borderline.
- Separate rule (from the books) from interpretation (yours).
- Do not use the chart to judge compatibility, hiring or health for third parties without their consent.
- Different schools weigh hidden stems, strength and structure differently; when two defensible readings differ, show both briefly.
